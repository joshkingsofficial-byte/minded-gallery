A colour study in six parts, inspired by Sanzo Wada's Dictionary of Colour Combinations. A meditative exploration of harmony, contrast and emotion.

A series of six concentric abstract compositions exploring colour relationships, contrast and emotional perception.

The designs use smooth colour fields without rhinestone or bead textures.

The colour relationships are informed by research into Sanzo Wada's Dictionary of Color Combinations.
