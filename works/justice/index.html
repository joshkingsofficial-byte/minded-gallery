A commentary on power, blindness and accountability.

An exploration of justice, blindness, power and accountability.

The concept uses a photograph of a sculptural figure holding a gold sword, with a red painted intervention across the eyes.

Proposed formats include a single skateboard deck and a nine-deck installation arranged in a 3 × 3 composition.

The underlying photograph was taken by Andre Pfeifer. Images of this work are withheld until the image rights are confirmed.
