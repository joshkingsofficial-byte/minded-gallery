About

The Saboteur and Curator of Accidental Errors

The artist

Dammy Ojo

working as MINDED

MINDED is the contemporary artistic identity of Dammy Ojo, a multidisciplinary creator working across photography, mixed media, sculptural objects, interactive technology, digital experiences and literary expression.

MINDED explores the relationship between intention and accident. The practice embraces experimentation, unexpected outcomes, material intervention and the discovery of meaning through creative play.

Art is not limited to one medium. A photograph may become a skateboard. A mirror may become an interactive artwork. An ordinary material may become a sculptural object.

Practice

  • Photography
  • Mixed media
  • Sculptural objects
  • Interactive technology
  • Digital experiences
  • Literary expression

Long-term vision

The immediate focus is an individual practice. Over time, MINDED is intended to grow into an independent art house: a place for guest exhibitions, curated collections, publications and collaborative installations.
